# Fortress in five minutes

> Fortress supports research and simulation. Scores, signals, Oracle decisions,
> and historical observations are not guarantees or personalized advice.

1. Open Fortress and choose **Login**, **Sign Up**, or **Guest**. Guest is the
   safest way to explore when it is enabled.
2. On **Dashboard**, check system status and the profile/recent-order summary.
3. Open **Stock Screener**. Select a universe. Searching for one symbol is
   read-only; **Run Scan** starts analysis and may be expensive, so do not run it
   merely to explore.
4. Read a result as evidence, not a prediction. **Fortress Score** is a
   rule-based 0–100 conviction summary. Open a result to see its components,
   data quality, Oracle decision (when recorded), and historical evidence.
5. Open **Scan History**, choose a scanner section, choose a dated run, and use
   **Back to Scan History** to return. Historical pages are read-only.
6. Open **Paper Trading** to review eligible signals and simulated open/closed
   positions. Creating or closing a paper trade changes saved simulation data;
   it does not place a broker order.
7. Explore **Mutual Fund Lab**, **REITs & InvITs**, **US Investing**,
   **Commodities**, or **Options** for asset-specific research.
8. Select **Help** in the lower-right corner, then **Take a Tour** (or
   **Restart Tour**) whenever you want the ten-step overview.

When a term is unfamiliar, use the [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md). Before changing
saved data, read the relevant section of the [User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md).
